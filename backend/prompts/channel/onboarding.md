👋 **Welcome to Project Anara, {sender}!**

To activate your autonomous agent, please connect your preferred AI model provider:

• **Option 1 (Web / Desktop UI):** Open the **Providers** tab in Anara Brain Console and add your API key (OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, Groq, DeepSeek, 9Router, or OpenRouter).
• **Option 2 (.env file):** Copy `backend/.env.example` to `.env` (or `%LOCALAPPDATA%\anara\.env`) and specify your provider and keys.
• **Option 3 (Local Ollama):** Run Ollama locally and set `OLLAMA_BASE_URL=http://localhost:11434/v1` in your `.env`.

Once configured, Anara will be fully activated and ready to assist you!
